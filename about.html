Biography

Pranav Sharma is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. Prior to joining IIT Delhi, he was a Researcher in the Power Systems Engineering Center at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory, now the National Laboratory of the Rockies, where his work focused on power-system stability, grid strength, renewable integration, and large-scale grid studies.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Iowa State University in 2022, where he worked with Prof. Venkataramana Ajjarapu and Prof. Umesh Vaidya on data-driven characterization and control of power-system dynamics. He received his B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Roorkee in 2015.

His research has been recognized with a Best Paper Presentation at the IEEE PES General Meeting, the NREL Key Contributor Award, and the Iowa State University Research Excellence Award.
