Research

Power System Dynamics & Stability

My research aims to develop the theoretical, computational, and practical foundations needed for the stable operation of evolving electric power systems. I combine power-system modeling and simulation, control theory, optimization, and data-driven methods while maintaining a strong connection with practical challenges faced by system operators and utilities.

Research Themes

Power System Dynamics & Stability

Understanding, quantifying, and mitigating voltage, frequency, and oscillatory stability challenges in modern power systems.

Grid Strength & IBR Integration

Scalable grid-strength assessment and analytical methods for the impact, sizing, and placement of grid-strengthening resources.

Measurement-Based Modeling & Monitoring

Koopman operator methods, modal analysis, parameter estimation, uncertainty quantification, and stability characterization using grid measurements.

Stability-Aware Planning & Control

Embedding stability requirements into planning, operation, and control of future grids.

Selected Directions
