Power System Dynamics & Stability

Pranav Sharma

Assistant Professor
Department of Electrical Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

Understanding and enabling stable operation of modern electric power systems.

My research focuses on the dynamics, stability, and reliable operation of modern electric power systems. I develop analytical, computational, and data-driven methods to understand and address emerging stability challenges associated with large-scale integration of inverter-based resources.

Research

My work spans power system stability and control, grid strength, renewable integration, large-scale system studies, and measurement-based modeling and monitoring, with an emphasis on translating fundamental methods into practical tools for grid operators and utilities.
