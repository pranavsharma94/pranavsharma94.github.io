Power System Dynamics, Stability, and Integration of Inverter-Based Resources

My research focuses on understanding and improving the dynamic behavior of modern electric power systems. I am particularly interested in power system stability, grid strength, integration of inverter-based resources, and modeling and analysis of large-scale power systems.
